📍 If you need to manually enter an address in your GPS:
The access road to the villa has no official street name. To enter an address manually into your car navigator, use the entrance corner reference point:
• Avenida del Cid, 481, 07198 Palma, Balearic Islands

Step-by-Step Visual Guide

1

Step 1: Turn at the Corner of Avenida del Cid, 481

Drive to Avenida del Cid, 481. When you reach the corner house (you will see number 481 painted on the wall and a green trash bin next to it), leave the main road and turn into the side street following the arrow.

2

Step 2: Main Entrance to Can Picornell

Continue straight along the side street for about 220 meters. Just before reaching the end of the road, on your right-hand side, you will see the large black metal entrance gate of Can Picornell with its stone sign post.
